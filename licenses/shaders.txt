slot's screen shaders are ports of shaders from the libretro shader collection
https://github.com/libretro/glsl-shaders

LCD3x by Gigaherz. Public domain.

Dot by Themaister. Public domain.
Changes: sharper dots (falloff 4.0) and a 0.85 blend.

zfast_lcd_standard by Greg Hogan (SoltanGris42), copyright (C) 2017.
GNU General Public License version 2 or later.

simpletex_lcd by jdgleaver, using the line weighting of zfast_lcd_standard.
GNU General Public License version 2 or later.
The paper texture (assets/paper.png, a 1024x1024 crop of textured_paper.png) is from the same shader.
Changes: the grid bias is 1.0, so black pixels carry no grid.

The card shaders in Shaders/ (Fragile):

Blend Grid.glsl by markbruno (markbruno/slot-plus, examples/shaders/blend-grid.glsl).
GNU General Public License version 3 or later.

GBA Color.glsl: the gba-color colour matrix and gammas, "Color Mangler" by hunterk, modified by
Pokefan531. Public domain. As used in Baggio94's slot-konkr presets.

LCD Grid V2.glsl: lcd-grid-v2 by cgwg. GNU General Public License version 2 or later.
Changes: the GLES 2 form without GLSL 1.30 arrays from Baggio94's slot-konkr, with the preset's
values (gain 1.5, gamma 2.2, colour 0.75), and the subpixel range measured in texels per output
pixel as the original does.

See-Through LCD.glsl: written for Fragile after roygravy/slot's See-Through Pixels.
GNU General Public License version 3 or later.
