# RG35XX Pro — Technical Specifications

- **Processor:** Allwinner H700 quad-core ARM Cortex-A53
- **Graphics:** Dual-core Mali-G31 MP2 GPU
- **Memory:** 1GB LPDDR4 RAM
- **Storage:** Dual MicroSD card slots (supports up to 512GB expansion)
- **Display:** 3.5-inch OCA-laminated IPS screen, 640 × 480 resolution, 4:3 aspect ratio
- **Battery:** 3200 mAh polymer lithium battery (approximately 6–7 hours of battery life)
- **Connectivity:**
  - Dual-band Wi-Fi (2.4 GHz / 5 GHz)
  - Bluetooth 4.2
  - Mini HDMI output
  - USB Type-C charging port
- **Operating System:** Linux-based stock OS; supports RetroArch and custom firmware such as Knulli
- **Controls:**
  - Classic D-pad
  - Four face buttons
  - Recessed dual analog sticks
  - Slanted L1/L2/R1/R2 shoulder buttons
